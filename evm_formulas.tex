\section{Gas Margin Ratio}

A transaction~\(T\) is flagged as \texttt{GAS\_LIMIT\_TOO\_LOW} when the ratio
of the user-supplied gas limit to the simulated gas consumed falls below 1.1:

\begin{equation}
  \text{GasMargin}(T) = \frac{G_{\text{limit}}}{G_{\text{used}}}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
  \text{flag}(T) =
  \begin{cases}
    \texttt{GAS\_LIMIT\_TOO\_LOW} & \text{if } \dfrac{G_{\text{limit}}}{G_{\text{used}}} < 1.1 \\[8pt]
    \texttt{OK}                   & \text{otherwise}
  \end{cases}
\end{equation}

The recommended safe threshold is \(\text{GasMargin} \geq 1.2\).

\section{Gas Utilization Percentage}

The percentage of the gas allowance consumed during simulation is:

\begin{equation}
  G_{\%}(T) = \left\lfloor \frac{G_{\text{used}}}{G_{\text{limit}}} \times 100 \right\rfloor
\end{equation}

where \(G_{\text{used}}\) is the gas consumed and \(G_{\text{limit}}\) is the
cap provided by the sender.

\section{Token Amount Normalisation}

ERC-20 balances are stored as unsigned integers on-chain. The human-readable
amount is recovered as:

\begin{equation}
  A_{\text{human}} = \frac{A_{\text{raw}}}{10^{d}}, \quad d \in \{0, 1, \ldots, 18\}
\end{equation}

where \(A_{\text{raw}} \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}\) is the on-chain integer value
and \(d\) is the token's \texttt{decimals()} value (default 18).

\section{Unlimited Approval Detection}

An ERC-20 \texttt{approve(spender, amount)} call is classified as an unlimited
approval when the approved amount equals \texttt{MAX\_UINT256}:

\begin{equation}
  \text{isUnlimited}(A) =
  \begin{cases}
    1 & \text{if } A = 2^{256} - 1 \\
    0 & \text{otherwise}
  \end{cases}
\end{equation}

\noindent where \(2^{256} - 1 \approx 1.16 \times 10^{77}\).

\section{Composite Transaction Safety Score}

Let \(\mathcal{W} = \{w_1, w_2, \ldots, w_n\}\) be the set of warnings emitted
for transaction~\(T\), each carrying severity
\(s_i \in \{\texttt{LOW},\, \texttt{MEDIUM},\, \texttt{HIGH}\}\).

Define indicator functions:

\begin{align}
  \mathbb{1}_{\text{HIGH}}(\mathcal{W}) &=
  \begin{cases}
    1 & \exists\, w_i \in \mathcal{W} : s_i = \texttt{HIGH} \\
    0 & \text{otherwise}
  \end{cases} \\[6pt]
  \mathbb{1}_{\text{MED}}(\mathcal{W}) &=
  \begin{cases}
    1 & \exists\, w_i \in \mathcal{W} : s_i = \texttt{MEDIUM} \\
    0 & \text{otherwise}
  \end{cases}
\end{align}

The composite safety score is:

\begin{equation}
  \text{Score}(T) =
  \begin{cases}
    \texttt{DANGEROUS} & \text{if } \mathbb{1}_{\text{HIGH}}(\mathcal{W}) = 1 \\[4pt]
    \texttt{WARNINGS}  & \text{else if } \mathbb{1}_{\text{MED}}(\mathcal{W}) = 1 \\[4pt]
    \texttt{SAFE}      & \text{otherwise } (|\mathcal{W}| = 0)
  \end{cases}
\end{equation}

The binary pass/fail outcome is:

\begin{equation}
  \text{passed}(T) =
  \begin{cases}
    \text{true}  & |\mathcal{W}| = 0 \\
    \text{false} & |\mathcal{W}| > 0
  \end{cases}
\end{equation}
